There is no catch. Our Dual Pricing model legally passes the cost of card acceptance to cardholders who choose to pay by card, rather than to you. Customers who pay cash pay your listed price. Card customers pay a slightly higher amount to cover network fees. This is 100% legal in all 50 states and is the same model used by gas stations for decades. You pocket 100% of your listed price every single time.
Yes. Dual pricing (also called cash discount or surcharging) is legal in all 50 states following federal court rulings. Each state has specific disclosure and signage requirements that differ. Our compliance team monitors all 50 states in real time and ensures your terminals, receipts, and in-store signage always meet current requirements. You are fully indemnified from compliance risk.
